# Stories for the low-poly story page (studio/lowpoly_video.py). Ours, written here: short "what if" premises told in
# big words, one or two at a time, on the beat, over a low-poly town (CC0 kits, studio/lowpoly/fetch.py).
#
# A story starts with "=== title". Each shot after it is one line: "@ SHOT [EFFECT] | words | words | ...".
# Each "| words" chunk is one caption, shown on a beat; a shot lasts as many beats as it has chunks (2 at least).
# SHOT is one of:
#   walk   a character walks toward the camera down a street          face   close on a character's face
#   town   high over the town                                          house  a house from the street
#   room   inside a room (kitchen, bathroom or bedroom: say which)     crowd  a street full of people
#   run    a character running away from the camera                    car    a car going by on a street
# EFFECT (optional) is one of: grow, shrink, float, shake, spin, blast, many, night, old
#   grow/shrink: the character changes size over the shot; float: rises off the ground; shake: the camera shakes
#   on every beat; spin: the camera circles; blast: things fly away from the character; many: the shot fills with
#   copies of the character; night: the sky goes dark.
# Cheeky is fine, nothing explicit, no real people, no jokes about religion, race or bodies, no asks.
