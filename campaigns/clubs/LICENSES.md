# Music licences

SPEC §4.4 — every track must be royalty-free. Baked-in commercial tracks get
fingerprinted, muted, or taken down, and repeat hits damage account standing.

Approved sources: Pixabay Music, Uppbeat (free tier), YouTube Audio Library,
Epidemic Sound (paid).

Note: the **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com → Audio Library) is a
free-to-use catalogue. It is not the same thing as downloading a song *from*
YouTube, which yields the copyrighted original and is what Content ID matches.
