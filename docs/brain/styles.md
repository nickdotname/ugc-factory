# Styles (renderers)

Back to [[README]]. A style is one script in `studio/` that turns `(song dir, seed, window)` into a finished
vertical video with the song as its sound. `studio/nightly.py` maps styles to scripts (`VISUAL` = picture styles
that can use any stretch of a song; `LYRIC` = styles that show the proven lyrics, so only well-timed stretches).
Every random choice inside a renderer goes through `studio/knobs.py` (`K.pick(name, options, rng)`), which records
it so [[learning]] can score it; `CQ_KNOBS` forces knobs.

## Shared conventions

- Output 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, the song's audio clip muxed in (`beat.audio_clip`).
- `beat.load(slug, t0, t1)` gives per-frame stem levels, kick/snare/hat frames and the beat grid; renderers move
  on those, not on guesses.
- **Finishing pass (2026-11-26, `studio/polish.py`).** Most drawn renderers wrap `Video.frame` with
  `polish.wrap(Video.frame, '<look>')` (math/race through `mathviz/kit.render`): bloom on coloured light (white type
  barely glows, `white=`), fine per-frame grain weighted to the mid-tones, a grade, a vignette, a slight red/blue
  split at the edges on some. One recipe per look in `LOOKS`; `CQ_POLISH=0` turns it off. Never on the illusion pages.
- **Real shaders (2026-11-26, `studio/gl.py`).** GLSL fragment shaders drawn off-screen through EGL + Mesa llvmpipe
  on the CPU runners (`moderngl`; studio.yml installs libegl1/libgl1-mesa-dri every night). ~30 fps at 1080×1920 for a
  light shader; `ss` supersamples. psy's fields are `studio/shaders/psy.glsl` at full size (was numpy at 432×768,
  still the fallback with `CQ_GL=0`).
- A `.json` beside each video records its knobs (and footage keys); nightly copies it into `recipes.json`.
- A credit line `♪ title — close quarters` near the bottom, above Instagram's UI.
- No engagement bait in on-screen text. Lyric styles show lyrics only where timing was verified.
- **Lyric sync (2026-11-24, `studio/sync_check.py`).** Every renderer that shows lyrics reads them through
  `sync_check.load_lines(song_dir[, own lines file])`, never `json.load('lines.json')`: a line whose sync is not
  proven (line_conf < 0.5, its own words off their vocal onsets, or its section failing the gate) comes back with every
  word at its first word's time, so it appears whole and never word by word; words past the end of the vocal stem are
  dropped. nightly gives a lyric style only sections that pass both `min_confidence` and the sync gate, and sets
  `CQ_LYRICS=none` for a picture style on any other stretch (vintage, lofi and cursor honour it too, own lines
  included). When picking the line on screen, **the latest line that has started wins**: taking the first match let a
  line lingering 0.25 s (cursor: 0.5 s) past its end hold back the next line, whose first word then showed ~0.28 s
  late on two line changes in three. Words ink `LEAD` (40 ms, in lines.json) plus up to 30 ms (the renderer's
  look-ahead) before they are sung; checked end to end on a muxed producer mp4 (ink within one frame of lines.json,
  audio starting 4 ms from the window start, ink a median 59 ms before the vocal onset).

## stopit
`studio/stopit_video.py`, on physics.bounce.lab (2 a night, from 2026-11-25). "Can you stop this?": one thing, one loop.
The camera drives down a low-poly street (numpy/OpenCV, no game footage, no Blender); a car, bollard, crate or cone
stands on the road; we hit it on a beat, it flips up ahead of us, crosses its own traced silhouette (the red line)
exactly once on a later beat, then passes over us and the loop restarts on an empty street. Two bars a loop; the
clip is whole loops. The aim is solved so the line sits clear of the title and the thing has left the picture
before the loop ends. Approved by Dylan from a sample. No "guess the plate" or comment asks.
Knobs: thing (car/bollard/crate/cone), sky, ask (the line under the title).
